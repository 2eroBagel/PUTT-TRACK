How to build a hole

  1. Path — click to drop track points; they snap into straight segments. The W slider sets track width.
  2. Depth / ramps — set the Y slider before placing a point to change its height. Points at different heights automatically become ramps. Higher points glow warmer, and ramp segments are drawn orange.
  3. New Path — starts a separate track piece (for splits or floating islands).
  4. Start and Cup — place your tee and hole. You need both.
  5. Ramp, Wall, Windmill, Sand, Boost, Water, Ferry, Disc, Gate, Current — click to drop each. Spin sets timing (windmills, movers, gates), Amt sets boost speed and current strength, and Dir rotates ramps, walls, ferries, gates, and currents.
  6. Erase removes just the piece you click; Undo reverses your last action; Pan drags the view.
  7. Test plays it now, Save keeps it in My Levels, and Code gives a share string to send friends (or paste one to load theirs).
